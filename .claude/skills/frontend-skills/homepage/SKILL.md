# Skill: Homepage — avtobirzhasi.kz

## Goal
Create a premium homepage that immediately explains both:
1. normal car marketplace search;
2. the unique Auto Exchange mechanism.

The homepage must not look like a generic classifieds template.

## Structure

### 1. Header
Wide and minimal.
Navigation:
- Автомобили
- Купить
- Продать
- Автобиржа
- Как это работает

Actions:
- Favorites
- Login/Profile
- Подать объявление

### 2. Hero
Large wide hero section.

Primary message should explain the product simply.

Example direction:
"Найдите автомобиль по вашей цене"

Supporting idea:
"Не ждите случайного звонка. Автобиржа автоматически сведёт покупателя и продавца, когда условия и цены совпадут."

Hero should include:
- strong H1;
- 1 short paragraph;
- 2 CTAs:
  - Купить автомобиль
  - Продать автомобиль
- premium automotive imagery.

Avoid placing too much text over the car image.

### 3. Quick search
Prominent search panel directly under or partially overlapping hero.

Fields:
- Регион
- Марка
- Модель
- Год
- Цена

CTA:
- Показать автомобили

Desktop: one horizontal row if space allows.
Mobile: stacked or 2-column layout.

### 4. Two ways to use the platform
Two large cards:
A. Купить сейчас
B. Купить через Автобиржу

The distinction must be immediately understandable.

### 5. Popular cars / Fresh listings
Large image-led grid.
Desktop:
- 3 or 4 cards per row;
- generous gap;
- not tiny cards.

### 6. Auto Exchange explainer
This is a signature section.

Explain:
- seller price decreases 1% daily;
- buyer price increases 1% daily;
- match near ±2%;
- both sides deposit 1%;
- contacts open after both deposits.

Use a visual price convergence diagram.

Do NOT overload the section with technical details.

### 7. Why avtobirzhasi.kz
3–4 benefits:
- automatic matching;
- real buyer and seller intent;
- transparent price movement;
- controlled deal flow.

### 8. Trust / safety
Explain deposits and contact unlocking clearly.

### 9. Final CTA
Strong wide section:
- Продать автомобиль
- Создать заявку на покупку

## Hero design rules
- wide-screen;
- high-quality car visual;
- large whitespace;
- no crowded filter wall;
- no more than 2 main CTA buttons.

## Cards
Card must show:
- photo;
- make/model;
- year;
- mileage if available;
- region;
- current price;
- optional Auto Exchange badge.

For exchange listings, additionally show:
- seller/buyer indicator;
- daily direction;
- simple movement info.

## Homepage tone
Confident and simple.
Do not write technical terms such as:
- cron;
- worker;
- price engine;
- background job.

Users should understand the concept without knowing implementation.
