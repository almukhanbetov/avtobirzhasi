# Skill: Vehicle Detail Page — avtobirzhasi.kz

## Goal
Create a premium car detail page with strong photography, easy-to-scan specs, clear seller/exchange state, and strong conversion.

## Desktop composition
Use a wide content container.

Top:
- breadcrumb or Back to cars;
- title;
- key metadata;
- favorite/share actions.

Main layout:
- large gallery: roughly 65–72% width;
- sticky price/action panel: roughly 28–35%.

## Gallery
Primary photo large and clean.
Additional thumbnails below or to the side.

Requirements:
- maintain vehicle image ratio;
- full-screen gallery modal;
- keyboard and swipe support where appropriate;
- lazy-load secondary images.

## Information hierarchy
Title:
Toyota Camry 2.5 Prestige

Metadata:
2022 • 42 000 км • Алматы

Main price:
17 850 000 ₸

Then:
- vehicle specs;
- description;
- seller information;
- safety information;
- similar cars.

## Sticky action panel
Normal listing:
- price;
- primary CTA;
- contact rules;
- seller summary.

Exchange listing:
- current moving price;
- direction;
- change rate;
- next calculation info if useful;
- CTA related to buyer request / exchange.

Do not expose protected contacts before backend allows it.

## Specs
Use structured grid, not a giant paragraph.

Examples:
- Год
- Пробег
- Двигатель
- Коробка
- Привод
- Кузов
- Цвет
- Руль

## Mobile
Move price/action card near the top.
Consider sticky bottom CTA.
Do not make users scroll several screens before seeing price.

## Trust
If listing participates in Auto Exchange, explain:
- why contact may be hidden;
- what Match means;
- deposit requirement;
- when contact will open.

Keep this explanation short and user-friendly.
